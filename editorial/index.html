Original stories. Accountable publishing.

NYC High Life Radio prepares original editorial coverage using source reporting and relevant primary evidence. AI tools may assist research, drafting and illustrations. We do not claim to have conducted interviews or witnessed events when we have not.

The owner has authorized automatic publication after factual and picture-rights checks. Missing evidence, disputed allegations or unclear image rights require further checking. Claims are attributed and qualified; invented quotes, facts and event photographs are not permitted.

AI-generated pictures are labeled as illustrations. Publisher photos require documented permission or an applicable license, including any required credit.

Source publisher names appear with each article. The newsroom retains evidence records privately. Neither source publishers nor illustrated subjects necessarily endorse our coverage.

World desk: source and photograph standards

The world desk prioritizes Reuters and Associated Press reporting alongside original evidence such as IPC analyses and UN agency reports. Reuters and AP are rated in Ad Fontes Media’s reliable analysis / fact reporting category as checked November 9, 2026. Those independent sample-based assessments guide source selection; they do not guarantee every article is correct.

Primary evidence, related reporting and older background are labeled separately. An official source is attributed as such, and multiple outlets quoting one report are not treated as independent confirmation. Each world brief retains its report date.

World news panels use real documentary photographs with credits and reuse terms. Archive and context labels stay visible. A photo from another date or place must never be presented as a photograph of the current event. No generated event photographs are used in the world desk. See the current sources and photograph notes.

Automatic reporting updates

The latest-reporting panels read the official Reuters and Associated Press YouTube feeds and check for updates every five minutes while the page is open. Publishers control the footage, preview images and player availability. Reports retain the publisher’s title and publication time; the feed is not rewritten by AI or individually reviewed by this station. Feed delays are labeled, videos older than seven days leave the current panels, and the direct publisher links remain available. “LIVE” in a title may refer to a scheduled or completed broadcast. We embed the official player rather than republish licensed photo files. These panels cover the publishers’ latest video reporting, not every article they publish.

Corrections and withdrawals

Wayne Kastro has full newsroom controls. Carl Tappin may view stories and take posts down through secure newsroom access. A withdrawal removes the story from the current feed and blocks its page on that deployment. Previously shared previews or older deployment snapshots may remain cached.

Automation and accountability

Some short briefs are generated automatically using public source reporting, exact evidence references, originality checks and a separate model verification pass. Automated checks can miss errors. These posts identify their automated byline. Uncertain or unsupported stories are held; owners can withdraw posts. Corrections are handled through the newsroom contact address.

Commercial independence

Advertisements, if enabled, must be identified and kept distinct from editorial stories and radio controls. Sponsored material must be disclosed. Advertisers do not receive a right to change independent reporting. We do not ask readers to click ads to support the station.
