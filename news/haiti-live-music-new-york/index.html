Haitian live music finds a New York connection

NYC High Life Radio follows BéLo’s New York appearance and the conversation around Caribbean music.

By NYC High Life Radio Editorial · 2026-10-15

Haitian music belongs in New York’s cultural conversation. NYC High Life Radio is following a performance report that connects a Haitian artist with a city audience.

The Haitian Times reports that singer-songwriter BéLo performed at Joe’s Pub on October 14, 2026. Its account describes a musical blend drawing on Haitian rhythms and other influences, with audience participation forming part of the evening.

Our editorial view: covering Caribbean culture means paying attention to the music itself as well as the headline appearance. This piece draws on the published account; NYC High Life Radio did not attend the show or interview the artist. Our accompanying artwork is an illustration, rather than a concert photograph.
