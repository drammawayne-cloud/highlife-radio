Brooklyn library puts teen creativity on its autumn calendar

Writing, journaling and seasonal crafts feature in Brooklyn Public Library’s October program ideas for young readers.

By NYC High Life Radio · Newsroom-reviewed brief · 2026-10-15

An October 14 Brooklyn Public Library post highlights creative activities for young readers, including writing sessions for teens and tweens, journaling and seasonal crafts. Its suggestions include a pen-pal club, cross-stitch and pumpkin-themed projects. These are program listings, rather than a report of events we attended.

NYC High Life Radio’s local perspective: a notebook or a hands-on project offers another way to make space for creativity alongside the music. Families interested in these activities should check the library’s current calendar for the particular branch, date, age range and registration details. This brief does not assume that every program is available at every location.
