Saint Lucia cricket: a return worth following

NYC High Life Radio puts women’s cricket and competitive opportunity in the spotlight.

By NYC High Life Radio Editorial · 2026-10-15

A return to competition can be an important sporting milestone in its own right. NYC High Life Radio is following a Saint Lucia cricket report that puts that moment in focus.

St Lucia Times reports that Nerissa “Breezy” Crafton made 116 before retiring not out during a match at Wenn Playing Field the previous weekend, following time away through injury. The account credits her contribution to Elizabeth William XI’s victory.

Our editorial view: consistent attention to women’s cricket should extend beyond a single standout score. Fixtures, opportunities to compete and continuing coverage help audiences follow a sporting journey over time. We have not independently interviewed Crafton, and this article makes no additional claims about her medical condition.
