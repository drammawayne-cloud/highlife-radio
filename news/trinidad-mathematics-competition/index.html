Trinidad and Tobago: mathematics moves into the spotlight

NYC High Life Radio follows the next stage of a secondary-school mathematics competition.

By NYC High Life Radio Editorial · 2026-10-15

Mathematics deserves a public stage alongside sport and entertainment. NYC High Life Radio is highlighting a Trinidad and Tobago school competition that brings problem-solving into view.

TTT Live reports that the Williams Educational Advancement Foundation’s 2026 MATHS UP MAX UP secondary-school competition has advanced to its quarterfinal stage. The announcement describes a quiz format intended to encourage mathematical thinking and teamwork.

Our editorial view: competitions can provide a useful reason to celebrate preparation and collaboration. That does not establish measurable educational outcomes on its own. The report does not provide a quarterfinal date, and we are not announcing results or a winner.
