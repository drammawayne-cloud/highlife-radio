Brooklyn families: bring better questions to the school search

NYC High Life Radio looks at a Brooklyn Public Library event built around researching public schools.

By NYC High Life Radio Editorial · 2026-10-15

Brooklyn families face an important research task when comparing schools: deciding which questions will reveal what daily learning looks like. NYC High Life Radio is highlighting a local opportunity to explore that process.

Brooklyn Public Library lists an October 15, 2026 session from 6 to 7 p.m., offered online and at Central Library’s Dweck Cultural Center. The listing describes reporters explaining how to use public information and prepare questions for school visits. The event is free, with registration requested.

Our editorial view: a single ranking cannot answer every family’s questions. Use data as a starting point, then ask about classroom support, communication and the student experience. This preview describes the library listing; it does not report that the event has taken place.
