Haiti / Food & livelihoods01

Haiti’s hunger crisis is projected to deepen

6.35Mpeople projected to face acute hunger, March–June 2027

The IPC estimates that 5.94 million people face crisis-level hunger or worse during September 2026–February 2027. Its projection rises to 6.35 million for March–June 2027. Insecurity, displacement, high food prices and climate shocks are driving the outlook.

Why follow thisThe later figure is a forecast, not a current headcount. Food access and support for farming families both deserve attention.

Source: IPC · Oct 28, 2026

Global / Climate & resilience04

Climate preparedness faces a major funding shortfall

$305Bestimated annual adaptation needs in developing countries by 2035

UNEP estimates developing countries will need about $305 billion a year for climate adaptation by 2035. Even the ambition to triple international public adaptation finance would cover only 20–29% of estimated needs, the agency says.

Why follow thisFor small island states and other exposed communities, the next question is how funding commitments become practical protection.

Afghanistan / Education & rights05

Millions of children remain outside formal education

53%of primary-school-age children outside formal general education

UNICEF and UNESCO report that 53% of primary-school-age children in Afghanistan are outside formal general education. Their report also says more than 2.6 million girls have been denied secondary education since 2021.

Why follow thisCommunity learning offers some access, but the agencies say it cannot replace the right to formal schooling.
