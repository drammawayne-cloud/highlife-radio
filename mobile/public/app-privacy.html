NYC High Life Radio · App support · App privacy

Mobile app privacy

Effective November 10, 2026. This notice describes the NYC High Life Radio mobile listener app. The app is operated by NYC High Life Entertainment. Contact: richrowrecords@gmail.com.

Listening without an account

You can listen without registering, providing an email address, or buying a subscription. The app does not record microphone audio, collect precise location or contacts, or include an analytics or advertising SDK. Commercials may be included in the station’s audio broadcast.

Radio, news and network connections

The app connects to the station’s configuration, Now Playing, news and audio services. These connections expose network information, including your IP address and request information, to the station and its hosting providers. Cloudflare provides app-preview and notification hosting; the station’s AzuraCast server supplies streaming audio and Now Playing information. Providers may process connection and security logs to deliver and protect their services. The app does not create a listener account or personal listening profile. This notice does not promise a fixed deletion period for provider security logs.

Optional iPhone live-show alerts

Alerts are optional and require your device’s notification permission. If you subscribe in the iPhone app, it sends an Apple push-notification device token and a random installation secret to the station’s alert service over HTTPS. The service stores your token, a SHA-256 hash of the installation secret, and the subscription time in Cloudflare D1. The secret is kept in your device’s secure storage. The token is sent to Apple to deliver a notification when a live DJ comes on air. No email address is required.

Use “Turn off live-show alerts” in the app to delete your subscription record. Delivery records, which contain the device token and delivery status, expire after 24 hours when the scheduled cleanup runs. Apple-invalid tokens are removed when Apple reports that they are no longer valid. You can also turn off notification permission in your device settings; doing that alone does not send an unsubscribe request to the station. Android live-show push alerts are not enabled in this release.

Your choices and requests

You can stop streaming with Pause and can use the app without subscribing to alerts. To ask about access, correction or removal of information handled by the station, contact the email above. We may need information to identify the relevant subscription or request; do not send passwords, verification codes or device secrets.

External websites and sharing

Website, news and support links open external destinations. Sharing passes the station link to the service you select through your device’s sharing interface. Those destinations handle information under their own policies. The station website has a separate privacy notice.

Children and updates

The app carries the station’s general broadcast and is not a service designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the station, contact us. We may update this notice when the app or its data practices change; the effective date identifies the current notice.
