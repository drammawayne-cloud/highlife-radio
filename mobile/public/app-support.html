NYC High Life Radio · App support · App privacy

Mobile app support

NYC High Life Radio is operated by NYC High Life Entertainment. For app support, email richrowrecords@gmail.com.

Start listening

Open Home and tap the large Play button on the radio. Use the bottom player to pause or resume while browsing the menu, station rotation and news. No listener account or payment is required. Audio starts only after you press Play.

No sound or interrupted audio?

Check your device volume and selected headphones or Bluetooth speaker. The stream needs an internet connection. If it drops, the app attempts a limited number of reconnects; pause and press Play to retry. Try another network if necessary. A “Live Broadcast” label is expected when a studio feed does not provide individual song titles.

Background listening

The native app is configured for background radio playback and system Play/Pause controls. Another audio app, a phone call, or disconnecting headphones may pause playback. Press Play again when you want to resume. Device power-saving and network restrictions can also affect streaming.

Mobile data

The shared stream is approximately 192 kbps, around 86 MB per hour before network overhead. Use Wi-Fi to reduce mobile data usage.

Free live-show alerts

In the iPhone app, use Subscribe to free live alerts and allow notifications. Tap an alert to open the app; press Play to listen. Turn alerts off in the app to unsubscribe. Android push alerts are not enabled in the current release. Alerts depend on the station connection, Apple delivery and device settings.

Send a useful support report

Include the app version, device model, operating-system version, approximate problem time, and whether you were on Wi-Fi or cellular. Do not include passwords, verification codes, stream credentials or device tokens.
