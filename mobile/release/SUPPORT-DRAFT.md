# High Life Radio support — owner review draft

Add the verified public support email or contact route before publishing this as the App Store support page.

To listen, open High Life Radio and tap Play. The live stream requires an internet connection. Check device volume, silent/audio settings, and any connected Bluetooth or headphones if you cannot hear the station. On iPhone, the app is configured for background listening and lock-screen controls while playing.

If the connection drops, the app attempts a limited number of reconnects. Pause and press Play to retry. Try a different network if the issue continues. Now Playing and named programming appear when the station publishes fresh information; missing metadata does not mean the audio stream has stopped.

The shared station stream is approximately 192 kbps, about 86 MB per hour before connection overhead. Use Wi-Fi if you want to reduce mobile data usage.

When requesting help, include the app version, iPhone/iPad model, iOS version, approximate problem time and whether Wi-Fi or cellular was used. Do not send passwords or verification codes.
