02 / SAVE YOUR SETUP

Connect your sound.

Your private connection pack gives you the station’s broadcast server, port, mount, and your own DJ username and password. Enter them in your approved broadcasting software and save the station preset.

03 / SOUND CHECK

Check in. Go live.

Join a soundcheck with the station before your first show. Check music, microphone and levels, then agree your start, handover and finish times.

When you are approved for your slot, open your saved station preset and connect. Disconnect at the agreed finish so the station can return to its rotation.
